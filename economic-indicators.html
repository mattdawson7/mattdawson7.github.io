Values are the latest published readings as of the snapshot date above. Month-over-month and year-over-year changes are computed from the source series; rates, spreads, the PMIs, sentiment and the unemployment rate are shown as point changes, everything else as percent changes. BLS published no October 2025 price or household-survey data during the federal shutdown, so year-over-year changes for CPI, core CPI and the unemployment rate run through September 2026 and are labeled “YoY (Sep).” This page is a fixed snapshot — it does not refresh.
